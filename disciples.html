Where It Began

The Origins of Christianity

From one life, a movement that spans the world.

c. 4 BC – 30 AD · Judea

Jesus of Nazareth is born, teaches, and is crucified and resurrected — the foundation of Christian faith.

c. 30 – 100 AD · The Early Church

His disciples carry the gospel across the Roman world, led by figures like Paul of Tarsus.

313 AD · Edict of Milan

Rome legalizes Christianity, ending centuries of persecution.

Today · A Global Faith

Over two billion believers, one shared hope in Christ.
